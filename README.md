🏍️ Bike Game – Basic Information

A bike game is a video game where the player controls a motorcycle and rides it through different roads, tracks, or environments.

Main features:

🏍️ Bike Control: Move the bike left, right, accelerate, and brake.
🛣️ Racing: Race against other bikes or complete a track within a time limit.
🏁 Levels: Different levels provide different tracks and challenges.
⭐ Score: Players earn points by completing levels, collecting items, or performing actions.
🚧 Obstacles: Avoid cars, barriers, and other objects on the road.
🪙 Coins/Rewards: Collect coins to unlock new bikes or upgrades.
🔧 Bike Upgrades: Improve speed, handling, braking, or other abilities.
🎮 Controls: Keyboard, touchscreen, or controller can be used.
